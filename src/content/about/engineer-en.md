I've been making software for more than fifteen years. At [**xarvio**](https://ag.xarvio.com/), BASF's digital farming platform,
I was part of (and often led) the team that processed and published geographic data: satellite imagery that ends up
in the maps telling farm machinery how much to apply and where. Like concerts, wheat doesn't move its dates however far
behind you are; there were rushes, complex problems, mistakes… but there were also results on the day.

Before that, as a freelancer, I helped build the platform for [**FAO**](https://www.fao.org/forest-monitoring/en) that lets
forestry specialists across Latin America publish their own monitoring data, and trained them to use it.

Most recently I was at [**geomatico**](https://geomatico.es), a cooperative where people matter above everything else.
From November 2026, I'm freelance again.
