Guitar is my instrument, classical and electric, both for more than twenty years. For more than a decade I've played in
concerts of choirs and amateur ensembles. I was called in to play, but I saw I could do more; soon I was
also on keys, writing the band parts, meeting the conductor to find where the arrangement could help the choir, and
handling cables, mics, chairs and the venue's technicians so the conductor had one less thing to think about on concert
day.

It became so natural that I ended up doing the same, this time purely backstage, for **Desluthiers**, a group that for
years staged tributes to *Les Luthiers*, slipping numbers of their own into the programme.
