Music came first: a guitar at fourteen, and soon after my first pieces, written with no idea what I was doing. Software
came later, at eighteen, and became my job.

I studied composition while working full time, a few subjects a year, and over those years each side shaped the other.

The engineer gave the composer a way of thinking: pitch, rhythm and dynamics as parameters, some free, some fixed. The
musician gave the engineer something harder to teach: you arrive at rehearsal knowing the music.

Whatever the job, I own it to the end, and the people I work with don't have to worry about it.
