From 2011 to 2022, themed concerts with Virelai, a youth choir, and the adult Orfeó Veus Juntes, each one a different
world: Queen, Brazilian music, singer-songwriters, the 60s, Mecano, and finally a musical with its own story. Some of
them were staged more than once, in other venues and years.

I took up the guitar to sound like Brian May. I never did, but I got to play Queen at the Palau de la Música, and 
along the way I learnt that writing the band's parts and running the sound check come down to the same job: making
sure the singers are heard.

- 2022 · **Maravillosamente extraordinarios, the musical** · A story of its own, with songs from well-known musicals (Virelai)
- 2019 · **MCN** · Mecano tribute (Virelai)
- 2015 · **The 60s** · Songs from the 60s and early 70s (Virelai, Divertimento: the choir school's instructors)
- 2013 · **Cantautor: La veu, el missatge** · Singer-songwriter songs (Veus Juntes)
- 2013 · **Queen on fire** · Restaging the Queen tribute at the Palau de la Música, Valencia (Virelai, Veus Juntes)
- 2012 · **Estrada do Sol** · Brazilian music (Veus Juntes)
- 2011 · **Queen at Quart** · Queen tribute (Virelai, Veus Juntes)
