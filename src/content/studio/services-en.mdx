- **Mockups.** Send me a score, a sketch or your DAW session, and I'll program, edit and mix it until it sounds played,
  whatever the line-up.
- **Orchestration.** From a melody or a piano sketch with a few notes on what you hear, I write it out for the whole
  ensemble, so it works in the mockup and on the players' stands.
- **Music to picture.** I write to the cut: the hits, the timings, the room the dialogue needs.
- **Scores and parts.** I prepare the score and the parts the players will read at the session, engraved like
  [my own](/works/turbulence-in-circles-score.pdf).
- **Software for your workflow.** I look for what your team still does by hand, then automate it or build the tool
  that's missing: that's [the engineer](/en/tech/) in me.
