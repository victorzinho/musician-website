For fifteen years I've written music for the idea someone already had. Right now it's a musical, *Yo mataré miedos por
ti*, whose recording I'm producing from the composer's scores, so it can be staged without live musicians. Before that
came [a commission](/en/works/ancho-es-el-mar/) for the Cor de la Generalitat Valenciana, the band's parts for [choir
concerts](/en/playing/#choir-concerts), an orchestral piece for a festival's call with a fixed line-up, and a degree in
composition.

And I've made software for even longer. Lately, tools like [imogen](/en/articles/imogen-orchestrate-by-prompt/), where
you can ask an AI agent for whatever you need in a score, like turning your DAW mockup into parts for the session
players.
