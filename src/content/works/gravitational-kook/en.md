I wrote *Gravitational kook* for a collaboration between the
[Conservatorio Superior de Música Joaquín Rodrigo](https://www.csmvalencia.es/) and the
[Instituto de Física Corpuscular (IFIC)](https://webific.ific.uv.es/web/). Each year, IFIC researchers gave us a talk on
a topic, we wrote music inspired by it, and it all came together in a science outreach event followed by a concert.

The year I took part, the topic was gravitational waves. What caught my engineer's mind was the effort behind finding
one: the signal is buried in noise, and the scientists have to filter the data over and over until they can finally
pick it out.

So I wrote the piece the same way. It starts as chaos: too much happening at once to make much sense of. As it goes
on, the layers clear and the main theme slowly comes to the surface.
